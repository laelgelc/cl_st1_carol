### Toulmin Argumentation Elements

* **C (`C_1` / `C_2`):** **Claim**
The core thesis, standpoint, or conclusion advanced by a speaker (Subscript `1` = Proponent / David; `2` = Challenger).
* **D (`D_1` / `D_2`):** **Data / Grounds**
Empirical facts, statistics, historical precedents, or specific observational evidence adduced to support a claim.
* **W (`W_1` / `W_2`):** **Warrant**
The general principle, bridge, or rule of inference justifying the cognitive step from the Data to the Claim.
* **B (`B_1` / `B_2`):** **Backing**
Explicit legal, institutional, statutory, or doctrinal authority certifying the validity of the Warrant (e.g., constitutional amendments, statutes).
* **Q (`Q_1` / `Q_2`):** **Modal Qualifier**
Lexical markers signaling the epistemic force, degree of certainty, or scope of the Claim (e.g., *explicitly*, *actually*, *absolutely*).
* **R (`R_1` / `R_2`):** **Rebuttal**
Counter-conditions, exceptions, or direct defeaters advanced to undercut, limit, or overturn the opposing argument's Data, Warrant, or Claim.
